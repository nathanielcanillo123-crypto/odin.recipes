CHICKEN ADOBO

  • Ingredients:

  • 2.5 to 3 lbs (approx. 1.1 to 1.4 kg) bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup cane vinegar or distilled white vinegar
  • 8 to 10 cloves garlic, crushed or minced
  • 1 medium onion, sliced (optional)
  • 3 to 4 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tablespoon whole black peppercorns (lightly crushed)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons brown sugar (optional, for balance)
  • 2 tablespoons neutral cooking oil (canola or vegetable)
  • 1 cup water
  • Steamed white rice and sliced green onions, for serving
    •

  • Instructions:

  • Marinate the chicken: Combine the chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, onion, bay leaves, peppercorns, and brown sugar in a bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or up to several hours for deeper flavor).
  • Reserve the marinade: Lift the chicken out of the bowl and set the marinade aside.
  • Sear the chicken: Heat the cooking oil in a large skillet or pot over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken pieces for about 4 to 5 minutes per side, then remove and set aside.
  • Simmer the sauce: Add the reserved marinade and 1 cup of water to the pot. Bring to a boil uncovered for 3 to 5 minutes without stirring to let the sharp vinegar mellow.
  • Cook until tender: Return the chicken to the pot, lower the heat, cover, and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and tender.
  • Reduce and serve: Uncover and simmer a few more minutes if you want a thicker sauce. Serve hot over steamed rice garnished with green onions.
