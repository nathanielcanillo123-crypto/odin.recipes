SPAGHETTI

Spaghetti is a long, thin, solid, cylindrical pasta that serves as a staple of Italian cuisine. Its name comes from the Italian word spaghetti ("little strings"), the plural diminutive of spago ("string"), while a single strand is a spaghetto.

  • Ingredients:

  • Pasta: 500 g spaghetti noodles, water, and salt for boiling
  • Meat: 500 g ground pork or beef, 6–8 hot dogs (sliced)
  • Aromatics & Base: 2 tbsp cooking oil, 1 medium onion (chopped), 4–5 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Sauce:500 g sweet-style spaghetti sauce (or tomato sauce), 1/4 to 1/2 cup sweet banana ketchup, 1–2 tbsp tomato paste (optional, for depth), 1 cup water or broth
  • Seasoning & Topping: 1 to 2 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste), salt and ground black pepper to taste, grated cheddar or processed cheese for topping
    •

  • Instructions:

  • The pasta: Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the spaghetti until al dente according to package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  • Sauté aromatics: Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the chopped onion and minced garlic until fragrant and softened.
  • Brown the meat: Add the ground pork or beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  • Add hot dogs: Stir in the sliced hot dogs and cook for 2–3 minutes until lightly browned.
  • Simmer the sauce: Pour in the spaghetti sauce, banana ketchup, tomato paste, and water or broth. Stir well, bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer uncovered for 15–20 minutes until thickened.
  • Adjust seasoning: Stir in sugar, salt, and pepper to taste. Add a splash of water if the sauce gets too thick.
  • Assemble and serve: Spoon the hot sauce over the cooked pasta (or toss them together), top generously with grated cheese, and serve warm.
