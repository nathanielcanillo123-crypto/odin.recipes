LASAGNA

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made from stacked, wide, flat sheets of pasta layered alternately with rich sauces, meats, cheeses, and vegetables.

  • Ingredients:

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 jar (24 oz) spaghetti sauce
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 container (15 oz) ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
    •

  • Instructions:

  • Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Boil lasagna noodles in salted water until al dente (or skip boiling if using oven-ready noodles). Drain and lay flat.
  • Make the meat sauce: Sauté chopped onion and minced garlic in olive oil until soft. Add ground beef (and optional Italian sausage or ground pork) and cook until browned, draining excess fat. Stir in crushed tomatoes or marinara sauce, tomato paste, salt, pepper, and herbs (like basil and oregano), then simmer for at least 15–30 minutes.
  • Mix the cheese filling: Stir together ricotta cheese, 1–2 beaten eggs, grated Parmesan, chopped parsley, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  • Assemble the layers: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Add a layer of noodles, followed by ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and shredded mozzarella. Repeat the layers, finishing with noodles, remaining sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan on top.
  • Bake: Cover the dish with foil (spray the underside with oil or cooking spray so cheese doesn't stick). Bake for 25–30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake uncovered for 10–25 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and lightly browned.
  • Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes so the layers set before cutting into squares.
